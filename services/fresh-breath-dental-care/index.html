Dental care questions

Good to know

Is this a replacement for a vet dental cleaning?
No — and we’re upfront about that. Our Fresh Breath Dental Care is a cosmetic, at-home maintenance service (brushing and a freshening treatment) that helps between professional cleanings. Deep cleanings, scaling under anesthesia, and dental health concerns should always go through your veterinarian.
How often should I book it?
For most pets, adding it to every regular groom (every 4–6 weeks) keeps breath fresh and supports gum health. Cats and dogs both benefit.
Can I book dental care on its own?
Yes. It’s $65 as a standalone visit, or you can add it to any dog or cat groom for the same price.
