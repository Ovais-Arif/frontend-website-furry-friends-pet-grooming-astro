Dog grooming questions

Good to know

Do you handle large or double-coated breeds?
Absolutely. From Yorkies to Goldendoodles to Huskies, our groomers are trained across coat types. De-matting and de-shedding are included — though heavily matted or specialty coats may take extra time (we’ll always tell you upfront).
How long does a dog groom take?
Most full grooms take 60–90 minutes depending on size, coat, and temperament. Because it’s one-on-one, there’s no all-day wait — your dog is done and relaxing at home before you know it.
My dog is anxious. Is mobile better?
Usually, yes. There are no other barking dogs, no cages, and no car ride. Just one calm groomer in a quiet van right outside your home. Most nervous dogs settle far faster than they would in a salon.
