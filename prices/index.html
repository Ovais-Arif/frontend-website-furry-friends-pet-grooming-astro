Pricing questions

What’s the difference between maintenance and one-time pricing?
Maintenance pricing is our lower rate for pets on a regular schedule (every 4–6 weeks) — consistent coats are faster and healthier to groom. One-time pricing applies to single visits with no commitment. Either way, there’s no contract and you can cancel anytime.
What is the 8% Service & Transportation Fee?
It’s a flat 8% added at checkout that covers bringing a fully-equipped grooming van — water, power, and supplies — right to your door. It’s shown upfront on every quote and is not a tax.
Are there any other hidden fees?
No. The price you’re quoted is the price you pay. De-matting and de-shedding are included rather than tacked on after, and any special-breed surcharge is confirmed before we arrive — never after.
When do I pay?
You pay after the groom is complete and you’re happy with the result, backed by our 100% satisfaction guarantee.
